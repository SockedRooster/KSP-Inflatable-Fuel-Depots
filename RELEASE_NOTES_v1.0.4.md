# InflataDepot 1.0.4 - Always-on crossfeed

Close KSP and replace the entire GameData/InflataDepot folder with this version.
Keep ModuleManager and B9 Part Switch installed.

Docking crossfeed is automatically enabled in the VAB/SPH and flight. The
Enable/Disable Crossfeed menu buttons and crossfeed action-group controls are
hidden. Older craft/vessels saved with depot crossfeed off are restored to on.
This controls the depot's own docking port; connected parts retain their settings.
The folded-tank capacity lock and empty-only flight deflation still apply.
VAB Inflate/Deflate and Original/Station Grey variants remain included.

Validation: plugin rebuilt against installed KSP/Unity assemblies. Existing
asset checks passed for all sizes. Config checks confirm always-on defaults
and VAB controls; models, textures and colour variant patch are unchanged.
ZIP integrity checked; no KSP or Unity libraries redistributed.
In-game verification remains pending: check VAB and flight menus/action groups,
load a craft previously saved with crossfeed off, and verify fuel transfer
through the deployed, docked depot. Also recheck inflate/deflate and colours.
