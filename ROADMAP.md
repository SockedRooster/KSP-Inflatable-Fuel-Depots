# RoosterWorks InflataDepot Roadmap

## Current release: v1.0.5

Completed: editor Inflate/Deflate controls, Original/Station Grey colour variants, always-on docking crossfeed, and permanently disabled docking-port staging. v1.0.5 was tested in KSP by the maintainer.

## Potential future work

- Additional fuel types: Monopropellant, Oxidizer-only, Xenon, and compatible third-party cryogenic resources.
- Balance review of mass, cost, technology placement, and deployment duration.
- Broader KSP and mod compatibility testing, including Kerbalism.
- Additional visual variants and localization.
- Save/load, docking, and action-group regression testing.

No future release schedule is committed. Report issues at https://github.com/SockedRooster/KSP-Inflatable-Fuel-Depots/issues.
