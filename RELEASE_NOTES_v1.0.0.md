# RoosterWorks InflataDepot 1.0.0

**Author:** SockedRooster  |  **License:** MIT  |  **Game:** Kerbal Space Program 1 (target 1.12.5)

RoosterWorks InflataDepot adds three compact deployable orbital fuel-storage parts
(1.25 m, 2.5 m, and 3.75 m). Launch them folded into a pancake, inflate them into a
cylindrical depot, and dock/refuel spacecraft at your station.

**Features**
- One VAB part per size, with B9-switchable LF/OX or Liquid Fuel-only capacity.
- Fuel capacity is locked until the tank is fully inflated. Inflation does not generate fuel.
- Deflation is blocked when either LF or Oxidizer remains in the tank.
- Functional Clamp-O-Tron-style docking interfaces and deployed-cylinder collision.
- RoosterWorks textured fabric, sealed tank end caps, Career unlock at Advanced Fuel Systems.
- VAB: Fuel Tanks; VAB Organizer optionally groups the tanks under Rocket Fuel.

**Requires:** B9 Part Switch and ModuleManager. Optional: VAB Organizer.

**Installation:** Download **`InflataDepot-v1.0.0.zip`** from **Assets** below;
extract into your KSP root directory. Remove any older `GameData/InflataDepot` first.

**Do not install** GitHub's automatic **Source code (zip)** as a player mod;
that archive has a different purpose. Install the named release asset above.

**Known caution:** Inflate in clear space to avoid collider overlap. Existing
craft action groups assigned to older beta Toggle actions may require reassignment.
